2. Description of Service

XTrack is a habit tracker with an expense tracker built in. It includes:

  • Habit Tracker: daily and weekly habits with reminders, streaks and statistics.
  • Challenges: habit challenges you take on together with friends, with daily check-ins and a leaderboard.
  • Finance Tracker: record income and expenses, automatically or by importing from bank and UPI SMS, or by CSV import; categories, tags, budgets, bills, recurring payments, accounts and credit cards.
  • Splits: share expenses with friends in groups and settle up.
  • Timers and Alarms: focus timer, workout/interval timer, loop timer, stopwatch, and full-screen alarms.
  • Backup: automatic daily backups on your phone, plus export and restore.
  • Roadmap: request features, comment and vote on what comes next.

Habits, finance, timers and alarms work without an account, and that data is stored on your phone. Splits, Challenges and Roadmap voting need an account, which you create by signing in with your email.

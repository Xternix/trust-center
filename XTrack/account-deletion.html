Delete Your XTrack Account

XTrack lets you create an account (via email sign-in) to use the Splits (expense-sharing) feature. You can request deletion of that account and its associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to xternix.dev@gmail.com from the email address used to sign in, with the subject line "Delete My XTrack Account".

We verify the request against the sign-in email and process it within 7 business days. You'll receive a confirmation once complete.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account and email address on our servers (Supabase).
  • Your Splits group memberships, shared expenses, and settlement history tied to your account.
  • Your profile name, if set.

What Is Retained

  • Expense records within a shared Splits group may be retained in anonymised or reassigned form so other group members' balances stay accurate, consistent with standard practice for shared-ledger apps.
  • Data required to comply with legal or fraud-prevention obligations, if any, retained only as long as legally required.

Data Stored Only On Your Device

Habits, finance entries, timers, alarms, and notes are stored locally on your device and are not part of this account. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes this data immediately — see the Privacy Policy for details.

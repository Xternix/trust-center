XTrack Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

XTrack is a habit tracker with an expense tracker built in. Most of what you put into the app stays on your phone. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what reaches our server, and why.

1. Information We Collect

Stored only on your phone

  • Habits: your habits, schedules, daily check-ins, streaks and statistics.
  • Finance: transactions, categories, tags, budgets, bills, recurring payments, accounts, credit cards and merchant-to-category rules. Transactions created from an SMS keep a copy of that message's text so you can see where they came from.
  • Timers, alarms and reminders: their settings and schedules.
  • Preferences: theme, what you track, and other settings.
  • Backups: the app keeps automatic daily backup files in its own private storage. A backup you export goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

Sent to our server, only if you sign in

You can use habits, finance, timers and alarms without an account. Signing in is needed only for Splits, Challenges and voting on the Roadmap.

  • Account: your email address (you sign in with a one-time code sent to it), your display name and your phone number. Your phone number is used so friends can find you and add you to Splits groups, and it can be linked to only one account.
  • Splits: groups you create or join, their members, shared expenses (amount, description, who paid, how it is split) and settlements.
  • Challenges: challenges you create or join (name, description, goal) and your daily check-ins.
  • Roadmap: feature requests, comments and votes you post.

Sent to our server automatically

  • Install record: when the app opens, it sends an anonymous install ID, the app version, your phone model and Android version (for example "Pixel 6a - Android 15"), and the network request's user-agent. On Android the install ID is a one-way, salted hash of the device's Android ID, so it cannot be turned back into the original ID. If you are signed in, the record is linked to your account. We use it to count active installs and see which versions are still in use.
  • Update checks: the app downloads a small version file from our website and checks Expo's update service for app updates (see Section 6). Like any web request, these reveal your IP address to the server that answers.

We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the app: habit tracking, expense tracking, budgets, timers, alarms and reminders, all on your phone.
  • To run shared features: keeping Splits groups, Challenges and the Roadmap in sync between the people who use them.
  • To keep the app up to date: telling you when a new version is available and delivering updates.
  • To understand usage: counting installs and app versions from the install record. We do not build advertising profiles.

3. SMS Data and the Finance Tracker

Recording your spending from bank SMS is a core feature of XTrack. It needs the READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS permissions, which you can grant or refuse.

  • Auto-detect: if you turn on "Auto-detect from SMS" (Settings › Finance tracker), the app checks each new SMS as it arrives, including when the app is closed, and records bank, card and UPI transactions it finds. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Import: "Import from SMS" reads the bank messages in your inbox for a date range you choose, so you can add past transactions.
  • What we use: only transaction alerts from banks, cards and UPI services. Other messages are checked against these patterns on your phone and are not stored.
  • On your phone only: SMS are read and processed entirely on your phone. Message text is never uploaded to our server or shared with anyone.
  • Your control: revoke the permission at any time in device Settings › Apps › XTrack › Permissions › SMS.

4. Device Permissions Explained

  • READ_SMS / RECEIVE_SMS: to record transactions from bank SMS. See Section 3.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: habit reminders, alarms and timer alerts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: to ring alarms and reminders at the exact time you set.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: to show a ringing alarm full-screen, including on the lock screen.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: to restore your alarms and reminders after the phone restarts.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: to finish processing a new bank SMS, and to keep timer sounds playing while the app is in the background.
  • WAKE_LOCK / VIBRATE / MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: to keep the screen on during a timer when you choose to, vibrate, and play alarm sounds at the volume you set.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: for app updates, the install record and the signed-in features above.

XTrack does not use your microphone, camera, location or contacts.

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • On your phone: data in Section 1 "Stored only on your phone" lives in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it, unless you have exported a backup.
  • On our server: account, Splits, Challenges and Roadmap data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase, and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access rules limit each person to their own data and the groups and challenges they belong to. We keep this data until you delete your account.
  • Signing out does not delete your habits or finance data from your phone.

6. App Updates

XTrack uses Expo's update service to deliver fixes without a full Play Store download. When the app checks for an update it contacts Expo's servers (expo.dev) and sends technical details such as the app version and platform, never your habits, finance data or SMS. The app also downloads a version file from xternix.in to tell you when a Play Store update is available.

7. Alarm Lock Screen Display

So you don't miss an alarm, XTrack can show a full-screen alarm over your lock screen when it rings. No data from other apps is accessed.

8. Data Sharing

  • No sale of data: we do not sell your personal information.
  • Other users: your display name, phone number, and the expenses and settlements you add are visible to members of the same Splits group. In a challenge, other members see your display name, check-ins and progress on the leaderboard. Your display name is shown next to Roadmap posts and comments.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in), Expo (app updates) and our website host (version file). They process data only to provide these services.
  • SMS data: never uploaded, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Legal: we may disclose data if required by law.

9. Children's Privacy

XTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data on your phone is protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock and encryption. Data sent to our server travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and database access rules restrict each user to their own records. No method is 100% secure.

11. Your Rights

  • Export your data: Settings › Export data saves everything stored on your phone to a file.
  • Delete data on your phone: Settings › Clear all data, clear the app's storage in device Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Delete your account: see our Account Deletion page to remove your account and server data.
  • Access or correct your data: edit your profile in the app, or email us.
  • Turn off SMS: turn off "Auto-detect from SMS" in the app, or revoke the permission in device Settings › Apps › XTrack › Permissions › SMS.
  • Turn off notifications: device Settings › Apps › XTrack › Notifications.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted here with a new effective date. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
